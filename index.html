Chavdêr Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

About this app

Chavdêr is a read-only public news monitoring app. It collects public news articles from websites and, only after Meta approval, public posts from Facebook Pages selected by the app operator.

Data processed

The app may store a public Page or website name, public post or article text, source URL, permalink, post identifier, and publication date. Names that appear within publicly published news text may appear as part of that text.

Data not requested

The app does not request private profiles, personal phone numbers, email addresses, private posts, direct messages, comments, reactions, or follower lists. It does not like, comment on, share, or publish content.

Purpose and storage

Data is processed only to display and review public news content in the Chavdêr dashboard. In the current version, records are stored in the app operator's local database and are not sold or publicly redistributed.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until the app operator deletes them through the app or removes the local database. For a data deletion or privacy request, contact: miqdad.zewki@gmail.com.
